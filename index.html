FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does “First see product, then pay” work?

We believe in total confidence and transparency: you see your product working first, test every interaction on a live staging preview, and pay only after you approve the milestone. Zero upfront risk.

How long does a typical project take?

Custom websites are typically delivered in just 2–3 days with live staging previews. Full SaaS MVPs, web applications, and mobile apps typically take 3–6 weeks. In week one we provide a concrete milestone breakdown.

Do we own the code and IP?

Yes, 100%. The code lives in your repository from the first commit and all IP transfers to you.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase?

Absolutely. We regularly embed with in-house teams, pick up existing codebases, and run an audit before making recommendations.

What happens after launch?

Every product includes 30 days of warranty support. For continuing operations, we offer monthly Website Maintenance & Care packages from ₹2,000 to ₹6,000 / month (covering 24/7 uptime monitoring, Core Web Vitals speed tuning, cloud backups, and regular code updates). If you choose not to subscribe to a monthly plan, any future updates or modifications cost just ₹500 per change on a pay-as-you-go basis.

Will our website be listed on Google, Bing, Yahoo, Yandex, and DuckDuckGo?

Yes, 100% guaranteed. Every website we build is configured with full technical SEO, dynamic XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and direct submissions to Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools (which syndicates to Yahoo Search and DuckDuckGo), and Yandex Webmaster. We verify that your website is actively listed and indexed across all 5 major global search engines.

Do you collaborate 100% online?

Yes, we operate 100% online and remote. All scoping, architecture, sprint demos, code reviews, and communication happen seamlessly online via Google Meet, Slack, and WhatsApp.

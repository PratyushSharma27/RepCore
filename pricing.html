FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you not take large upfront deposits?

We have complete confidence in our engineering ability and speed. By letting you inspect working software on staging previews before releasing payments, we eliminate founder anxiety and build authentic long-term partnerships.

What payment methods do you accept?

For Indian clients, we accept direct NEFT/RTGS bank transfers and UPI with official GST invoices. For international clients, we accept wire transfers (SWIFT) and Stripe in USD, EUR, or GBP.

Do you sign an NDA before we share project details?

Yes, 100%. We respect your intellectual property and routinely sign mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to the first discovery call.

Who owns the source code and designs?

You do. All source code, Figma assets, database schemas, and intellectual property belong strictly to your company from the first commit.

Can we change or add features mid-project?

Yes. Because we work in agile two-week sprints with transparent communication, we can easily swap, adjust, or reprioritize backlog items as you receive feedback from early users.

Will our website be listed on Google, Bing, Yahoo, Yandex, and DuckDuckGo?

Yes, 100% guaranteed. Every website we build includes complete technical SEO, dynamic XML sitemaps, semantic Schema metadata, and verified submissions to Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools (which syndicates to Yahoo Search and DuckDuckGo), and Yandex Webmaster. We verify that your website is actively listed and indexed across all 5 major global search engines.

What if I don't take a monthly maintenance plan? How much do changes cost?

If you don't take a monthly care plan, any future updates or modifications will cost ₹500 per change on an ad-hoc basis (such as text edits, image swaps, banner tweaks, or minor styling updates). Our monthly maintenance plans (₹2,000 to ₹6,000 / month) offer significantly more value by bundling multiple hours of changes, continuous speed tuning, 24/7 uptime monitoring, security patches, and automated cloud backups.

What ongoing support and maintenance is provided after launch?

Every build includes 30 days of warranty support. For continued peace of mind, we provide dedicated Monthly Maintenance & Care plans starting at ₹2,000 to ₹6,000 / month. These include 24/7 uptime monitoring, continuous Core Web Vitals speed tuning, automated cloud backups, security patches, and monthly developer hours with zero long-term lock-in.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does “First see product, then pay” work?

We build with complete confidence and trust: you see and test your product live first, and pay only after you approve the working demo. For all fixed-scope projects, we share interactive live staging builds. Once you test every flow and approve the milestone, you make the payment. Zero upfront risk.

How long does a typical project take?

Custom websites are delivered in just 2–3 days with full responsiveness, SEO, and guaranteed listing on Google, Microsoft Bing, Yahoo Search, Yandex, and DuckDuckGo. Complete SaaS MVPs, web applications, and mobile apps typically take 3–6 weeks. You get a concrete milestone breakdown in week one.

Do we own 100% of the code and intellectual property?

Yes, unconditionally. All code, design assets, and intellectual property belong to you from day one in your own GitHub repository.

Who will I be working with directly?

You work directly with founder Pratyush Sharma and senior engineers. No account managers or junior developers passing tickets back and forth.

How are payments structured?

Fixed-scope projects are billed in milestone-based installments in Indian Rupees (₹) via bank transfer, UPI, or Razorpay/Stripe. Retainers are billed monthly with 30-day flexibility.

What happens after launch?

We provide full documentation, deployment walkthroughs, warranty support, and flexible ongoing retainer options as your user base scales.

Can we discuss under an NDA?

Yes. We regularly sign mutual NDAs before reviewing proprietary ideas, codebases, or product specs.

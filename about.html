The Anti-Agency Manifesto

Why we dismantled
the traditional agency playbook.

Most design and development agencies are organized around billable hours, middle managers, and holding client code hostage. RepCore Studio operates on 4 non-negotiable laws.

Law 01

Inspect Before You Pay

We believe founders should never be forced to wire 50% deposits on blind faith. We build your milestone releases on live staging URLs first. You test every flow, verify responsiveness, and pay only after you are 100% satisfied.

Milestone Validation Guarantee

Law 02

Founder Engineering Only

Big agencies pitch you with their senior partners, then hand your codebase off to offshore juniors or unpaid interns. At RepCore Studio, founder Pratyush Sharma personally designs, architects, and programs every single component.

1:1 Direct Technical Access

Law 03

Sovereign Day-One Code Ownership

You never have to beg for your codebase. From commit #1, all code lives directly in your private GitHub organization with signed IP assignment. If you ever hire an in-house team, they can clone and run it in 5 minutes.

Zero Vendor Lock-in

Law 04

48-Hour Staging SLA

Agency bureaucracy kills startup momentum. Instead of wasting month-long cycles in PowerPoint meetings, we ship an interactive staging URL within 48 hours of sprint kickoff, delivering continuous momentum.

Rapid Staging Velocity
